## Overview

We build ubiquitous and wireless sensing systems that turn everyday radio signals — such as mmWave radar, RFID, Wi-Fi, and UWB — into rich sensors for people, objects, and environments. By jointly designing wireless hardware, signal processing, and learning-based models, we push the limit of what radio signals can perceive, from locating and tracking targets to understanding human behaviors, interactions, and environment states.

We further explore the intersection of wireless sensing with embodied AI and robotics, where pervasive RF perception equips intelligent agents with a complementary sense that preserves privacy and stays robust where cameras fall short — through occlusion, darkness, and beyond the line of sight.
