## Overview

We design next-generation wireless network and communication systems that make connectivity faster, more efficient, and more pervasive — spanning cellular networks, mmWave links, Wi-Fi, and ultra-low-power backscatter connectivity for IoT devices.

Through careful measurement studies and the co-design of the physical layer, protocols, and hardware, we improve the spectrum efficiency, power efficiency, and reliability of wireless links, and we validate our designs with practical systems built on real radios and testbeds.
