## Overview

We create battery-free and ultra-low-power IoT systems that let everyday objects compute, communicate, and sense. Leveraging technologies such as RFID, backscatter, and UWB, our designs enable a single radio signal to simultaneously deliver power, data, timing, and sensing to passive devices, removing the battery from the equation entirely.

By co-designing RF hardware, modulation schemes, and protocols, we push passive connectivity toward longer range, higher throughput, and richer capability, and we validate our designs with prototypes built on real hardware. Together, these efforts make maintenance-free smart devices practical at scale — in homes, warehouses, hospitals, and cities.
